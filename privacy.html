Privacy

What this site collects, and why

This site collects two things: anonymous usage data through Google Analytics, and the details you choose to send through an enquiry form. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Usage data

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read and how people find the site. It records:

  • pages viewed, and links and buttons clicked
  • approximate location (city or region)
  • device type, browser and screen size
  • the site or search that referred you

Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We have turned off Google Signals and all advertising features, so this data is not linked to your Google account or used to target ads. It sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_<ID>, to tell repeat visits apart.

To opt out on every site that uses Google Analytics, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Enquiry forms

When you send an enquiry, the details you enter (name, role, institution, email and any notes) go through Web3Forms to our inbox. We use them only to reply to you and to follow up on that conversation. Web3Forms does not keep a copy beyond 30 days.

Contact

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email info@julianstevenson.com.
