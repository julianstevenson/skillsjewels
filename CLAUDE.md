## Rules that matter

**Don't convert these pages into a normal React app.** Each page is a Claude Design export: an `<x-dc>` template with a `<script type="text/x-dc">` logic block, parsed and rendered at runtime by `support.js`. It looks like something that wants refactoring. It isn't. Work within the existing structure.

**Don't touch `_ds/`.** That's the generated "Industry" design system the pages were built against. Override its tokens in the page's own `<style>` block instead.

**Don't delete `.nojekyll`.** Without it, GitHub Pages runs Jekyll, Jekyll skips underscore-prefixed directories, and the entire `_ds/` folder stops serving. The site deploys with no styling and the cause is not obvious.

**Layout lives in inline `style` attributes.** Responsive behaviour therefore uses `@media` blocks in the `<helmet>` `<style>` that override `sj-`-prefixed classes with `!important`. Breakpoints are 900px and 560px. A new two-column section needs `class="sj-split"` on its wrapper or it stays two-column on a phone.

**Page content doesn't share a stylesheet.** Each page carries its own `<style>` block in its own `<helmet>`. A colour change has to be made in every page (plus the flier's own block, and `site.css` for the nav and footer). The one shared stylesheet is `site.css`, and it covers the nav and footer only.

**The nav and footer are generated by `site-chrome.js`, not pasted into pages.** Every page has `<div id="site-nav" data-cta-href="…">` immediately after `<body>` and `<div id="site-footer"></div>` immediately after `</x-dc>`. They sit *outside* `<x-dc>` on purpose: `support.js` replaces `<x-dc>` with a React root and anything inside it is React's to re-render. Menu entries live in the `MENU` object in `site-chrome.js`. An entry without `href` shows as "Soon". To publish a new page, give its entry an `href`. That is the only place nav and footer links are defined. Each page's `<noscript>` link list inside `#site-nav` is the exception, and it's kept short on purpose.

**`site.css` overrides `#dc-root`'s height.** `support.js` sets `#dc-root` to `height: 100%`. With the footer after it, that would make the footer overlap the page, so `site.css` sets it back to `auto`. Don't remove that rule.

**Google Analytics is set up in `site-chrome.js`.** The GA4 Measurement ID is the `GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` constant at the top. While it's the `G-XXXXXXXXXX` placeholder, gtag.js isn't loaded. Consent Mode v2 is on: analytics storage granted, all ad storage, signals and personalisation denied. Events: `generate_lead` (fired in each page's form success branch through `window.sjTrack`), `cta_click`, `nav_click` and `contact_email_click` (all from one delegated click listener). Mark `generate_lead` as a key event in GA4. `privacy.html` describes what GA collects. Keep it in step if the GA setup changes.

**Photo heroes.** Employment Services has a headline photo: the header is wrapped in `<div class="sj-photo-hero">` with a Sapphire gradient over `assets/hero-employment-services.jpg`. Its text is Chalk, the primary CTA is Chalk on Sapphire, and the evidence panel is a Chalk card, so the brand figure colours still read. Copy that wrapper when Tertiary Education and Digital get photos. Each photo hero page also gets its own `share-<page>.jpg` crop.

**Industry and Services pages follow one template.** Employment Services and Tertiary Education are the reference. In order: a breadcrumb (`.sj-crumbs`), a two-column hero with a `.blueprint` evidence panel, `#why` (problem on the left, "What SkillsJewels does" with four numbered steps on the right), `#related` ("Where this connects", three `.blueprint` cards in `.sj-related`, with an unbuilt page shown as a muted card with a Soon badge), `#enquire` (the dark form band), then the page note. Build a new page by copying one of the two and replacing the content, not by designing a new layout. When a related page goes live, swap its muted card for a linked one on every page that lists it.

**The flier is not responsive, deliberately.** It's an A4 print artefact.

## Brand

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — headings, CTAs, dark section | Sapphire | `#1B3A5C` |
| Background | Chalk | `#F7F5F0` |
| Links, secondary emphasis | Amethyst | `#5B3A73` |
| Data highlight | Emerald | `#0B5D3B` |
| Sparing accent only | Topaz | `#C89B3C` |

Headlines: Source Serif 4, 600. Body, forms, data: Public Sans — the same family used across Australian government digital services, chosen deliberately for a DEWR/ATEC and TAFE audience.

`.blueprint` and its four `<i class="corner">` children come from the design system: square corners, hairline border, registration marks. Don't round them, don't drop the corner marks.

## Writing copy

Five voice pillars: direct, systems-fluent, evidence-led, non-bureaucratic, quietly ambitious. Name the structural problem plainly. Back claims with real figures, not adjectives. Short sentences.

Never use: leverage, seamless, unlock, empower, journey, revolutionise, disrupt, game-changing, simply, just, easy, exclamation marks.

Figures on the site are indicative and need checking against current DEWR reporting before the site goes in front of a government or provider audience. Don't invent or adjust a caseload number to make a sentence work — flag it instead.

## SEO

Every page carries its own title, meta description, canonical link, Open Graph/Twitter card tags and JSON-LD in the real `<head>` (not `<helmet>`). Facebook, LinkedIn and Slack unfurlers don't execute JS, so anything meant for link previews has to be in the raw HTML, not compiled in at runtime. Keep titles to about 60 characters and descriptions to 150–155.

- **Indexing:** a page is `index, follow` and in `sitemap.xml`, or `noindex, follow` and out of it, never a mix. Draft-copy pages, `privacy.html` and the redirect stubs are `noindex` (see README's SEO table).
- **Structured data:** `index.html` has the `Organization` with `"@id": "…/#organization"`. Every other page has an `@graph` with a `BreadcrumbList` (Home › page) and a `WebPage`, `AboutPage` or `ContactPage` whose `publisher` points at that `@id`. Services pages add a `Service`. A new page copies the block and changes the names and URLs.
- **Share images:** link previews use `assets/share-brand.jpg` (1200×630, `summary_large_image`) everywhere except the home page, which uses `assets/share.jpg`. Industry pages switch to a crop of their own hero photo once they have one.
- **noscript fallback:** each page has a `<noscript><main class="sjc-noscript-main">` after `#site-nav`, repeating its h1 and intro. If you change a page's h1 or intro, change the noscript copy too.
- **Redirects:** GitHub Pages has no server-side redirects. A moved page becomes a stub: `noindex`, canonical to the new page, meta refresh, plus `location.replace` to keep the `#anchor`. `workforce-development.html` and `product-consulting.html` are stubs.

`robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` point at the same live domain as everything else — see the CNAME item under Known outstanding.

## The enquiry form

Posts to Web3Forms, which relays to an inbox. The access key in the page is public by design. The destination is set by the key, not by the address shown in the footer. Don't "fix" the footer address expecting mail to follow it. See README for the full wiring.

## Deploying

Push to the default branch. Pages redeploys in about a minute. There is no staging environment, so preview locally first:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Commit in small, single-purpose commits. This site has no tests and no build, so `git revert` is the safety net.
