When you need this

A board or investor needs an independent view of a product organisation's strategy, team or delivery before a funding or acquisition decision.

A CEO or new product leader wants someone outside the organisation to test their thinking before they commit to it.

A major decision (a platform rebuild, a restructure, a senior hire) needs to be made well, and made once.

How the engagement runs

01

Frame. Agree the question, who needs the answer, and by when.

02

Review. Read the material, talk to the people involved, and look at the product and how it's built.

03

Advise. Give a clear recommendation with the reasoning and the risks, in writing and in the room.

04

Follow through. Stay on as counsel while the decision is carried out, on a retained basis if useful.
