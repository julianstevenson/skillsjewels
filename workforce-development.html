Why upskilling doesn't stick

Internal L&D and off-the-shelf short courses move at the speed of the business, but produce nothing that counts beyond the organisation — no credit, no portability, no signal a future employer or a university will recognise.

Universities hold the assessment rigour and credentialing authority employers trust, but course design cycles and generic curricula rarely match a live workplace problem or a project team's timeline.

The result: a growing post-experience learning market — professionals reskilling mid-career for AI, data, digital and sustainability roles — is served by everyone except the institution best placed to make it count.
