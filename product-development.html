SkillsJewels takes a product from agreed direction to working software: discovery to confirm what to build, delivery to build it, and a team set up to keep going after we leave.

When you need this

A new product or major feature has been approved, but the team that will build it doesn't exist yet or is stretched across other work.

Delivery is moving, but nobody is checking whether what ships changes the measures it was meant to move.

Discovery happens once at the start and then stops, so the product drifts from what customers need.

How the engagement runs

01

Frame. Translate the strategy into outcomes, measures and the riskiest assumptions to test first.

02

Discover. Test those assumptions with customers and prototypes before committing to a full build.

03

Build. Deliver in short cycles, with working product and a measure check at the end of each.

04

Transfer. Hire or develop the roles that own the product, and hand over the process with the code.
